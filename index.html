Hermes personal

A private, self-hosted mail connector for its account owner. This application is not offered to the public.

What the connector does

It connects the owner's personal assistant to the owner's mail accounts. Expanded Google access uses the Gmail API with gmail.modify permission. At the owner's request, the connector supports all folders, bounded search and reading, drafts, sending, flags, moving messages, and creating folders or labels.

Searches cover an explicitly selected date window of at most 31 days, with pagination and coverage information. Reading returns bounded plain text, headers, and attachment descriptions. It does not automatically download attachments or follow links.

Data handling

OAuth credentials are kept in a separate restricted service on the owner's server. The assistant does not receive raw credentials. Deleting mail or folders and moving mail to trash are not exposed. Received mail is untrusted content and does not authorize actions.

Requested message text may be processed by the model provider powering the owner's personal assistant. The assistant may retain requested results in its private conversation history. This public information site contains no mailbox data, assistant history, configuration files, or credentials.
