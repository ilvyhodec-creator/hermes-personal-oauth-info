Privacy policy

Hermes personal is a private mail connector operated by its sole account owner. It is not a public sign-up service.

Google data accessed

The connector requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify to support the owner's requested mail management. The previous read-only grant remains usable for reading until the owner grants the expanded permission. Access covers all folders, including spam and trash. At the owner's request, the connector can search and read messages, create drafts, send messages, mark messages, organize them into folders or labels, and create folders or labels. The connector does not expose message deletion or moving messages to trash. Attachment descriptions may be returned; attachments are not separately downloaded and email links are not automatically followed.

Purpose and sharing

Requested account identity, headers, message text, and connection status are returned to the owner's personal assistant and may be processed by the model provider powering that assistant to answer the owner's request. OAuth secrets are not provided to the assistant or its model provider. Message text is untrusted data and is not treated as an instruction to operate the server or disclose secrets.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Its use of Google API information adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored outside public code and knowledge files in a restricted server-side store. Access is separated from the personal assistant and working agents. Credentials and raw provider errors are excluded from application logs. Connection checks do not change mailbox flags.

Retention and control

Credentials are retained while the owner keeps the connection configured. The owner can revoke Google access in Google Account third-party connections and request deletion of the dedicated local and server-side configuration. Revoking permission and deleting stored credentials are distinct actions. The connector does not maintain its own message-content archive. Requested results may remain in the personal assistant's private conversation and tool history, subject to that deployment's history and backup settings. Email content is not automatically published to a knowledge base or this information site.

Contact

For this single-user deployment, privacy requests are made by the account owner through the existing private Hermes conversation or to the operator who maintains the connection. The support email is also shown on the Google consent screen.

This information site

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no application analytics, sign-in form, advertising, or client-side tracking code. GitHub may process technical access information under its privacy statement. No mailbox content is published here.
