Terms of use

Hermes personal is a private, self-hosted mail connector intended solely for its account owner. There is no public registration or subscription offered by this deployment.

Authorized use

Use is limited to accounts the owner has authorized. The Google integration requests gmail.modify permission for mail management. The connector supports all folders, bounded search and reading, drafts, sending, message flags, moving messages, and creating folders or labels. Deleting messages or folders and moving messages to trash are not exposed. Instructions embedded in received messages do not authorize sending mail or other actions; the owner's requests determine the authorized work.

Availability

Connections depend on the mail provider, network, and the owner's server. The connector does not guarantee uninterrupted access or permanent authorization. The owner may disable or revoke a connection at any time.

Limits and changes

Search results describe the folder and date window covered; they do not claim to cover the entire archive. Large messages may be truncated or rejected with an explicit error. Attachments and links are not automatically opened. Automated schedules or notifications require separate configuration by the owner. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this page and the privacy policy.
