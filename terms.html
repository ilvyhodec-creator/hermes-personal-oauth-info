Terms of use

Hermes personal is a private, self-hosted mail connector intended solely for its account owner. There is no public registration or subscription offered by this deployment.

Authorized use

Use is limited to accounts the owner has authorized. The current Google integration requests Gmail read-only permission and exposes only account identity and connection checks. The current interface does not read messages, send mail, change flags, or delete mail.

Availability

Connections depend on the mail provider, network, and the owner's server. The connector does not guarantee uninterrupted access or permanent authorization. The owner may disable or revoke a connection at any time.

Changes

Any expansion to message retrieval or analysis must be separately implemented and disclosed before it is enabled. This page and the privacy policy will be updated to reflect such changes.
